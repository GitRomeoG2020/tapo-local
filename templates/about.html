About

A local dashboard for a Tapo H500 and the devices around it.

This project is a local-first dashboard for TP-Link Tapo H500 hubs. It lists battery cameras and their hub recordings, opens live view through go2rtc, and controls other Tapo devices such as plugs and lights.

The account and hub address stay on your machine. The dashboard talks to the hub on your LAN. It does not send those recordings to a cloud service of its own.

This is an unofficial project. It is not affiliated with TP-Link. A firmware update can change how the hub answers, so treat the local behavior as something that may need a code update later.
